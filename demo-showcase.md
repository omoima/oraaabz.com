# Demo showcase

Concept sites built to show prospective clients what their website could be. Each demo is served at an unlinked, `noindex` route on oraaabz.com. This file is tracked in Git and never built into the site.

The repository is public, so anything written here can be read by anyone, including the client. Keep it to what you'd be happy for them to see.

### What the demo shows

- **A clear first action.** "Book a discovery flight" and "Call operations" sit above the fold, on any phone.
- **The full training path.** PPL → Night → Instrument → CPL → Instructor, so a visitor can see where one lesson could lead.
- **The fleet as a selling point.** New, South African–built Sling 2s with glass cockpits.
- **Charter as its own service,** with a direct line to the 24/7 operations desk.
- **A four-step "Plan your first flight" enquiry.** Goal, experience (or passengers, for charter), timing, then contact details. In the demo it ends on a summary and sends nothing.
- **An FAQ** answering the questions new students ask first: age, medical, how long it takes, and English proficiency.
- **Contact, directions and socials** in one footer, with tap-to-call and tap-to-email links.
- **Fast and accessible.** A static page of about 20 KB of gzipped CSS and JS. It supports keyboard navigation and respects reduced-motion settings.

### Placeholders to replace with Flitecare

### Pitch

A modern, mobile-first site that turns visitors into discovery-flight bookings and charter calls, and that Flitecare can keep updating themselves. Next step: a 20-minute call to confirm their courses and collect photos, then the live build.
